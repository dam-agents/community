# Complaint policy

This policy is how anyone in the community raises a concern and has it tracked, monitored, and escalated until it is resolved.
It applies across every repository in the `dam-agents` organization.

A complaint is taken seriously, acknowledged, and either resolved or escalated.
No concern is left to disappear in a thread.

## What counts as a complaint

A complaint is a concern about how the project or its community operates that is not already covered by another channel.
Examples: an unanswered or mishandled contribution, a maintainer decision you believe was unfair, a pattern of behaviour that is unwelcoming, or a process that is not working.

Some concerns have a dedicated path and should go there first — they are monitored more closely and, where relevant, handled privately:

| If your concern is… | Use instead |
| --- | --- |
| A bug or a feature request | [dam issues](https://github.com/dam-agents/dam/issues) |
| A security vulnerability | [`SECURITY.md`](SECURITY.md) — report privately |

If you are not sure where a concern belongs, file it as a complaint and we will route it.

## How to file a complaint

Open an issue in the [`dam-agents/community`](https://github.com/dam-agents/community/issues) tracker.
Include:

- What happened, with links to the issue, pull request, or discussion if there is one.
- What outcome you are looking for.
- Whether anything about it is time-sensitive.

Please describe behaviour and decisions rather than attacking a person, and keep to what you observed.

If the complaint is about a specific maintainer and you do not want to raise it in public, open a minimal issue asking for a private channel (with no details), and the other maintainers will handle it.

## How complaints are monitored

Complaints are actively monitored, not left to chance:

- The [`dam-agents/community`](https://github.com/dam-agents/community/issues) issue tracker notifies the full [maintainer group](GOVERNANCE.md#maintainers); it is watched, not owned by one person.
- A maintainer **acknowledges** every complaint within **3 business days**, even if only to say it has been received and who is looking at it.
- The complaint stays open, with its status visible, until it is resolved or explicitly closed with a reason.
- Resolution is recorded in the issue so the outcome is on the record.

## Escalation path

If a complaint is not acknowledged within the window, or you are not satisfied with how it was handled, escalate:

1. **Ask for escalation in the issue.** Comment that you are escalating and why. This notifies the whole maintainer group.
2. **Request a maintainer-group review.** The maintainers review the complaint as a group rather than leaving it to the person it concerns, and respond with a decision and reasoning.
3. **Conflict of interest.** If the complaint involves a maintainer, that maintainer recuses themselves from the review.

Maintainer decisions are made in the open and recorded.
Because the project is maintained by a [group](GOVERNANCE.md#maintainers) rather than an individual, escalation always has somewhere to go, and no single person is both the subject of a complaint and its sole judge.
